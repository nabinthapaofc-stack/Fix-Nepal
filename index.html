About Us

Fixनेपाल is a civic-tech platform built to expose and fix the problems that slow Nepal down. Potholes, blocked roads, delayed ambulances, broken infrastructure. These issues cost lives every single day. We’re here to kill the excuses and bring real visibility to what’s broken.
Our job is simple:
You report. Authorities see it. Action gets taken faster.
We combine real-time reporting, location tracking, and community-driven data to make Nepal’s streets safer and more reliable. No politics, no drama just problems and solutions.
FixNepal exists because the system won’t improve unless someone pushes it.
We're that push.

What We Do?

Report Potholes

Bad roads destroy cars, delay ambulances, and cause crashes. We let you drop a pin, upload photos, and submit the report in seconds. Authorities get accurate data instead of vague complaints, so repairs actually happen.

Request Pothole Ambulance Support

When you need an ambulance, every second counts. FixNepal shares your exact location instantly, reducing the "Where are you?" confusion that wastes precious minutes. Faster location = faster rescue.

Track Issue Status

No more complaints disappearing into thin air. Every report has a timeline: submitted, viewed, assigned, and resolved. You always know what's happening, who's handling it, and whether the job is actually done.
